## No games included

PokeDaisy comes with no games, no BIOS files, no console keys and no game art. It never downloads them, links to them or tells you where to find them. You play ROMs you dumped yourself from cartridges you own.

The pictures the companion shows from a game (the party menu's art, the region map, the trainer card, the heroes for `MAKEOVER`) are rebuilt on your device from your own copy of that game, and stay there. PokeDaisy only knows where to find them; it carries none of them.

PokeDaisy is never sold, and nothing in it is locked behind a payment. Supporting it on Ko-fi, Patreon or Buy Me a Coffee is optional and unlocks nothing in the app.

## Where we host anything

- **This website**, pokedaisy.com: the manual, the release notes, every release's files and its source code, and the
  ROM check (which reads a ROM in your browser and never uploads it).
- **The feedback form**, which stores what you write so the developer can read it. Nobody else can read it back.

## Reporting content you own

If you own the rights to something you see on a PokeDaisy site or service and believe it's there without your permission, send a notice to `legal@pokedaisy.com`. We answer within a few days, and if the notice is complete we take the content down while we look into it.

To act on it, we need:

1. Your name, address, phone number and email.
2. What the work is that you own (or act for).
3. Exactly where the content is: its address on the site, or the account and file.
4. A statement that you believe in good faith the use isn't allowed by you, your agent or the law.
5. A statement that the notice is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, that you own the right or may act for the owner.
6. Your physical or electronic signature.

This follows the US Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA), and we handle notices sent from anywhere the same way.

A game's maker can also ask for PokeDaisy to stop supporting their game. We've done it before: the game then plays as a plain game, without the companion's features, and the website leaves it out ([Games removed at their makers' request](supported-games.md#games-removed-at-their-makers-request)).

## Counter notice

If something you put on a PokeDaisy service was taken down and you believe that was a mistake, send a counter notice to the same address with:

1. Your name, address, phone number and email.
2. What was removed and where it was.
3. A statement, under penalty of perjury, that you believe in good faith it was removed by mistake or misidentified.
4. That you accept the jurisdiction of the federal court for your district (or, outside the US, any district where we may be found), and accept service from the person who sent the notice.
5. Your physical or electronic signature.

We pass it on to the person who sent the notice. Unless they tell us within 10 to 14 business days that they've gone to court, the content can come back.

## Repeat infringers

Anyone who keeps putting other people's copyrighted files on a PokeDaisy service loses access to it, and what they stored there is deleted. The same goes for anyone who sells Pokémon or saves through it, or uses it to bother other players.

## Your privacy

What the app sends over the internet, and when, is in [What does PokeDaisy send over the internet?](troubleshooting.md#what-does-pokedaisy-send-over-the-internet). The website has no accounts, ads or cookies, and only counts visits, anonymously.
